## Project Name
Pompom Sequence

## Final concept
A tiny, deflated pompom lies on the ground. You press and hold it until it puffs up, drag it to a launch spot, then pull it back like a slingshot and toss it into a ring. I wanted it to feel soft and playful, like handling something almost alive.

## Your custom tool panel(s)
*Image: `screenshots/0-panels.png`*

I made two foldable panels. The Motion panel tunes the feel: hold time, drag responsiveness, gravity, bounce, friction, ring size and motion curves. The Visual panel tunes the look: background, paper texture, fiber colors and thickness, ring colors and shadow. I used them to find the balance between light and heavy, and to turn my grey studies into one visual world.

## Concept evolution
I started with three grey studies: Hold, Drag and Flick. My professor pointed out that Drag and Flick felt like the same gesture (press, move, let go). So I replaced Flick with Throw: you pull back and release like a slingshot, and the pompom arcs through the air. I also added a launch spot and a target ring, so the end of Drag now leads straight into Throw.

## Final Prototype Reflection
The panels let me feel changes instantly instead of guessing in code. I was surprised how much a few values, like gravity, bounce and hold time, shape the pompom's personality, and how much color changes its sense of weight. I chose the lighter purple version because it fits the soft, playful mood best. The smooth hand-off between steps feels most resolved. Next, I'd add matching sounds and test it with other people.
